Pregnancy Education and Birth Preparedness

Hello. This is a simple health education page. It is about learning what happens in pregnancy and how to get ready for the birth.

It is for people who are pregnant, and for partners or family who want to learn as well.

This page is especially for immigrants and ethnic minorities who do not know how the health system works. We will explain things in a simple way, like who to see, where to go, and what to ask.

What you can learn here

  • How to look after yourself in pregnancy
  • What to expect as the weeks go on
  • Signs that mean you should get help
  • What to pack and who to call when labour starts
